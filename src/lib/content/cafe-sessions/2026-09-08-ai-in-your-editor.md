## Session Summary

The opening session of the new Coding Café year, on working with an AI assistant inside the editor you already use rather than in a browser tab. We looked at what moving the assistant into the IDE actually changes, got Copilot running in VS Code, and spent the second half on hands-on work and discussion.

## Topics Covered

- Why the IDE — the three scenarios, the copy-paste tax, and the trade you are making
- A tour of VS Code: ghost text, inline chat, the chat view, and the context system
- Worked examples: comment-first completion, explaining inherited code, good versus bad prompts, and tests you were never going to write
- R and RStudio — you are not required to switch editors, and getting R working in VS Code
- FAIR and the safety net: documenting your process and the five git commands that make this safe
- Verifying output — "it runs" is not "it is right" — and what actually leaves your machine
